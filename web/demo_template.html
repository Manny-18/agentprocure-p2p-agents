Architecture, evidence, business case

Three tools, each doing the job it is best at.

LangGraph runs the case

A stateful graph with conditional routes (block, reject, exception, partial release), a human approval interrupt() with checkpointing, and one audit record per agent step.

CrewAI runs sourcing

A crew of three: Category Manager (Kraljic), Supplier Performance & Risk Analyst (scorecard), Negotiation Strategist (should-cost). Offline it is driven by a scripted expert LLM over the same ReAct loop.

n8n runs the business plumbing

Request intake, approval e-mails with approve/reject links that resume the graph, Google Sheets audit log, and a daily MSME 45-day payment watch, all through a FastAPI service.

graph TD
  start([Purchase request]) --> intake[Procurement Analyst]
  intake --> policy[Policy and Compliance Officer]
  policy -- block --> controller[Finance Controller]
  policy -- clear --> crew[[CrewAI sourcing crew]]
  crew --> approval{{Human approver - interrupt}}
  approval -- reject --> controller
  approval -- approve --> po[Buyer issues PO]
  po --> ap[AP Specialist - 3-way match]
  ap --> tax[GST and TDS Specialist]
  tax -- exceptions --> resolver[Exception Resolver]
  tax -- clean --> treasury[Treasury Analyst]
  resolver -- partial release --> treasury
  resolver -- hard stop --> controller
  treasury --> controller
  controller --> done([Case memo and audit trail])

Numbers come from tools, never from the LLM

The LLM explains, prioritises and drafts. Every amount, date and rate is computed by tested rules. A guardrail rejects any LLM text that contains a figure not present in the tool outputs, and the crew cannot overrule the scorecard winner; only the human approver can.

Rules encoded (as of 10 October 2026)

  • TDS on goods: Income-tax Act 2025 s.393(1) Table Sl. 8(ii) (formerly s.194Q): 0.1% above Rs 50 lakh per seller per tax year; 5% without PAN; buyer turnover above Rs 10 crore.
  • MSMED Act s.15: pay micro/small suppliers within the agreed term, never more than 45 days from acceptance (15 days with no written agreement); s.16: compound interest at 3x RBI Bank Rate (5.75% after the 7 Oct 2026 MPC decision, so 17.25%); s.23: interest not deductible.
  • Income-tax Act 2025 s.37(2)(g) (formerly s.43B(h)): late MSE dues are deductible only in the year paid.
  • GST 2.0 rates from 22 Sep 2025 (5% / 18% / 40%); IGST for inter-state supply; e-invoice IRN for suppliers above Rs 5 crore AATO; IRN within 30 days for AATO of Rs 10 crore or more; ITC reversal if the supplier is unpaid after 180 days.
